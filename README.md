One sentence, under 200 characters, on the best advice I ever got: my father's rule for judging people by how they treat the waiter.

> My father once told me, 'Never trust a man who is nice to you but rude to the waiter,' and twenty years later I still judge people by it.

Running `main.py` writes that sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
